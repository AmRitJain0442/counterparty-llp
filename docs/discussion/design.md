# Design council

The four desks share an intake, evidence collection, opposing counsel and a decision ledger. A desk records what each source supports, its observation time, and any alternative explanation. A separate claim-level gate controls the verdict. Gemini can draft an explanation from minimized observations. It has no signing authority.

The interface should follow the reference site's map-to-record interaction. Selecting an investigation desk opens its evidence path. Selecting a case opens the underlying witnesses and explains why a finding survived or was killed. Estate movements need origin, destination and cohort size. Small or undated cohorts stay unknown. Never imply that an animation is provider telemetry.

The development discussion has separate adjudication and infrastructure proposals in this directory. Sonnet owns the stage interface, Sol the provider workflows, and the 6.2 design workers the rules and ledger. Outbound integrations use explicit inputs and server-side credentials. The local service binds to loopback; a hosted installation needs private authenticated ingress.
