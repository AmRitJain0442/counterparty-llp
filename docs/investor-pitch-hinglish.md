# Counterparty LLP samjhein aur pitch karein

Jab kisi supplier ke baare mein koi aisi baat kahi jaaye jis par paise ka faisla tika ho, Counterparty dikhata hai ki kis source ne kya dekha, kab dekha, aur faisla lene se pehle kya poochhna baaki hai.

## Investor ke saamne aise bolenge

“Kal ek supplier ka contract renew hona hai. Meeting mein koi kehta hai, ‘Inki team chhoti ho rahi hai.’ Procurement waale company profile, headcount ka chart aur jobs ki list le aate hain. CFO poochhta hai, ‘Ye teen alag jagahon se pata chala hai, ya teeno mein ek hi source ki baat dohraayi gayi hai?’

“Isi sawaal ke liye humne Counterparty LLP banaya. Humne Crustdata par hi ek live investigation chalaayi. Crustdata ke estimate mein 19 October aur 2 November 2026 ko 47-47 profiles thi. Uske jobs index mein 25 postings thi. Chhe evidence cards bane. Jab humne unka source dekha, saare Crustdata tak hi pahunche. Case ka result tha **Insufficient witnesses**. Ab buyer supplier se ek seedha sawaal poochh sakta hai, ‘In dono dates par is business unit mein kitne log kaam kar rahe the? Kya aap uska record share kar sakte hain?’

“Hum pehle un procurement teams ko bechna chahte hain jo supplier renewals sambhaalti hain. Aapse do vendor-risk leads se introduction chahiye. Har team apne teen public suppliers ki ek-ek claim chune. Phir dekhenge review mein kitna waqt laga, committee ke saamne woh kya dikha paaye, aur kya agle renewal ke liye woh pay karenge.”

## Apni team ke liye paanch sawaal

**Customer Crustdata pehle se use karta hai. Counterparty ke liye kyun pay karega?** Crustdata se company ki pehchaan, headcount ke estimates, jobs aur employment history milti hai. Counterparty us data ko buyer ke sawaal se jodta hai. Reviewer har finding ki date aur source khol sakta hai, dekh sakta hai ki kisi doosre record se bhi us baat ki tasdeeq hoti hai ya nahin, aur apna faisla usi file mein likh sakta hai.

**Itne agents ka kya faayda hai?** Chaar groups mein 12 lead agents hain. Har lead ke saath do specialists hain. Ek specialist dekh sakta hai ki do records ek hi company ke hain. Doosra dekh sakta hai ki dono ek hi time period ki baat karte hain. Map par unke sawaal, findings aur handoffs dikhte hain. Agar saare 36 agents ek hi record padhein, toh evidence ka source phir bhi ek hi hai.

**Aur kahaan kaam aata hai?** Company desk par supplier ke headcount aur hiring ki jaanch hoti hai. Payment desk par vendor ka bank account badalne ki request check hoti hai. Candidate desk par consent lekar employment claims check hote hain. Estate desk par Norway mein kisi company ke bankrupt hone ke baad dekha ja sakta hai ki available records mein log agli job ke liye kahaan gaye.

**Live case chalaane aur purana case kholne mein kya farq hai?** Naya case relevant providers se us waqt ka data maangta hai. Upar ka link kholne par wahi evidence aur timestamps dikhte hain jo us case mein file hue the. Us collection mein 2.23 Crustdata credits lage the. Replay par dobara collection charge nahin lagta. Gemini aur hosting ka kharcha alag hai. Hosted ledger sab visitors ko dikhta hai, isliye pitch ke liye public supplier ka example use karein.

**Pilot mein kya naapenge?** Pehle ek claim par buyer aaj kitna waqt lagaata hai, woh note karenge. Phir Counterparty ke saath wahi kaam karke dekhenge ki kitna waqt laga, reviewer kin baaton ka saboot dikha paaya aur kaunse sawaal khule rahe. Per-case pricing isi pilot mein test karni hai.
